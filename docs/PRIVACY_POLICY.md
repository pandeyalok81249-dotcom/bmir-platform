# Privacy Policy — AI Resume Intelligence & Career Platform

**Effective Date:** November 4, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 4, 2026

## 1. Introduction
AI Resume Intelligence Platform ("we", "our", or "us") provides privacy-first career advancement tools, including resume optimization, evidence graphing, predictive trajectory simulations, and mock interview coaching.

We treat your personal career data with the highest standard of confidentiality. This Privacy Policy discloses what information we collect, how it is used, and how your privacy rights are safeguarded under global regulations (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA).

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
- **Account Credentials**: Name, email address, password hash (salted using Argon2id).
- **Career Documents**: Uploaded resumes (PDF, DOCX, TXT), cover letters, job descriptions, and user profile preferences.
- **Interactive Responses**: Mock interview answers, recorded STAR self-evaluations, and trajectory simulation role targets.

### B. Automatically Collected Technical Data
- **Device & Connection**: IP address (anonymized for rate limiting), operating system, browser/app version.
- **Usage Metrics**: Feature interactions, subscription quota consumption, and audit timestamps.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- To parse, structure, and audit resume quality against evidence-grounded benchmarks.
- To generate tailored career roadmaps, job fit scoring, and STAR interview coaching drills.
- To administer your subscription tier and enforce monthly quota limits.
- To monitor platform reliability, prevent fraud, and mitigate abuse.

---

## 4. AI Processing & Zero-Model-Training Guarantee
- **Zero Training on Private User Data**: Your uploaded resumes, parsed career history, and interview answers are **never** used to train, fine-tune, or improve foundational AI models (such as OpenAI GPT or Google Gemini).
- **Sub-Processor Zero-Retention Agreements**: Third-party LLM providers process structured prompts statelessly under strict Enterprise Data Processing Agreements that prohibit data persistence.

---

## 5. Data Security & Storage
- **Encryption in Transit**: All data transmitted between client devices, our API, and databases is encrypted using TLS 1.3.
- **Encryption at Rest**: Stored documents and relational records are protected using AES-256 encryption.
- **Tenant Isolation**: Strict row-level tenant boundary validation prevents cross-user document or workspace leakage.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion
Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to:
- **Access & Export**: Download a full JSON export of your resume data and profile history.
- **Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten)**: Delete your account and all associated documents at any time via Settings -> Account Deletion. Upon deletion, all resume files and database records are permanently purged within 30 days.
